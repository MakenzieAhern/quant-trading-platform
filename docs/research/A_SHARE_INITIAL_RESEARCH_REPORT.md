# Initial A-Share Research Report

Status: **NOT EXECUTED**  
Official OOS: **OUT-OF-SAMPLE NOT YET RUN**

The research implementation, methodology and reporting schema exist, but no production A-share
dataset has passed the readiness gate. Consequently Development, Validation and OOS result sections
contain no metrics, charts or profitability claims. This is intentional: corporate-action
normalization, delisted-security coverage and complete historical rule exceptions are material
unresolved sources of bias.

When readiness passes, this report will record providers/retrieval/checksum/coverage, market model,
strategy and bias controls; Development and Validation results; explicitly approved one-time OOS;
CSI300 comparison; exact performance metrics, equity/drawdown/monthly/yearly diagnostics, turnover,
costs, closed-trade win rate, profit factor, positive-month and positive rolling-12M ratios; known
limitations and the persisted reproduction command. Undefined metrics will be `null`.
