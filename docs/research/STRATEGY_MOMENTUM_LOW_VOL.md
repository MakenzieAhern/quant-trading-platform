# Momentum and Low-Volatility Strategy

**ID:** `cn_a_cross_sectional_momentum_low_vol_v1`

**English:** China A-Share Cross-Sectional Momentum and Low-Volatility Strategy

**中文:** 中国A股横截面动量与低波动策略

This is a long-only research strategy, not a profitability claim. It combines twelve-to-one-month
momentum, price versus 120-session trend and inverse 20-session volatility. It rebalances every 20
valid sessions into the top score decile, at most 20 equal-weight holdings, with lot rounding and
residual cash. ST inclusion policy, costs, liquidity thresholds and other settings must be frozen
before any official experiment. No weight, threshold or rebalance-frequency optimization is part
of version 1.
